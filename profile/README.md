# Hi there, welcome to brezel.ai 👋

brezel.ai builds practical AI-powered tools for extracting, processing, and working with online data.

## Our Portofolio

### [Clean AI Watermark](https://cleanaiwatermark.com/)

**Remove the AI watermark. Keep the text.**

Clean AI Watermark removes watermarks and hidden characters that are used to identify AI-generated text, while keeping the original meaning, tone, and length as close as possible to the original.

---

### [BrezelScraper](https://brezelscraper.com/)

**Extract business data from Google Maps at scale.**

BrezelScraper lets you search for businesses by location and category and extract **50+ data fields per place**, including business names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, websites, opening hours, ratings, reviews, photos, and enrichment data.

Run scraping jobs from the dashboard or connect BrezelScraper to your workflows through its **API, webhooks, n8n, and Zapier** integrations. Export your results as **CSV, JSON, or directly to Google Sheets**.
